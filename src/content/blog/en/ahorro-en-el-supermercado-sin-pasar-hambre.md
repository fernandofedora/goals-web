If every time you leave the supermarket you feel you spent too much, you are not alone. Most of us walk in with a list and walk out with twice as much.

## Plan before you enter

What has worked for me is checking what I already have in the fridge and pantry the day before. That way I avoid buying what is already there.

### Make a realistic list

Write only what you will use in the next 7 days. Include simple meals you know you will prepare.

## Compare prices without wasting time

Check store-brand products. They often have the same quality as name brands but cost less.

Watch out for “2-for-1” deals. Only buy if you will actually use it before it expires.

## Change small habits

Buy fruits and vegetables in season. They are cheaper and go further.

Something worth trying is going to the local market one day a week instead of everything at the supermarket. The price difference on some items is noticeable.

## Before you close this tab

If you feel the grocery bill gets out of hand every week, it is not because you cannot control yourself. It is that no one ever taught you how to plan purchases according to your real life and family.

*The trick is not to buy less — it is to buy what you will actually use.*
