Look, if by the time payday arrives you're already counting the days until the next one, you're not alone. A lot of people go through the same thing and it's not because you're overspending on whims.

The reality is that prices rise faster than salaries and that complicates everything.

## Start by looking at the truth without filters

Before thinking about saving, you need to know exactly where every córdoba goes. It's not about judging yourself, but seeing the full picture.

Write down everything you spend for a week: the bus, lunch out, the quick coffee, even your phone top-up. What has worked for me is using a piece of paper or a note on the phone. At the end of the week you'll see patterns you didn't notice before.

## The trick is to separate even the minimum

You don't need to reserve 20 % right away. Start with 5 % or even less. The important thing is that it becomes automatic.

If you get paid by transfer, ask the bank or use a simple app that moves that amount to another account as soon as the salary comes in. That way you don't even see it and you don't have to decide every month.

Watch out for this: if you can't do it one month, don't punish yourself. Just try again the next month. Consistency beats quantity.

## Change small habits without feeling deprived

Sometimes it's not the big salary that's missing, but the small expenses that add up. Instead of buying lunch out every day, prepare something simple twice a week. That difference shows up by the end of the month.

That said, it's not about stopping living. It's about choosing where it's worth spending and where it's not.

## Look ahead even if it's just a little

When you already have something saved, no matter how small, your mindset shifts. You start seeing that it really is possible. And that gives you strength to continue.

If you live alone or help at home, adjust the amount according to your real situation. There's no single rule.

## Before you close this tab

If you've been feeling for months that your salary never stretches and that saving is something only people who earn more do, it's not your laziness. It's that no one showed you how to do it with what actually comes in each month.

_The first córdoba you set aside, even if it's tiny, is already proof that you can start changing things._
